About This Course

This comprehensive Quran memorization course is designed for students at all levels. Whether you're just starting your Hifz journey or looking to improve your existing memorization, this course provides a structured, step-by-step approach with expert guidance.

You'll learn proven memorization techniques used by renowned Huffaz, establish a sustainable daily practice routine, and receive personalized feedback to ensure correct pronunciation and Tajweed.

What You'll Learn

Proven memorization techniques (Active Recall, Spaced Repetition)
Proper Tajweed rules for beautiful recitation
How to build a sustainable daily memorization schedule
Tips for retaining and reviewing previously memorized sections
Understanding the structure and themes of the Quran
Self-assessment methods to track your progress

Requirements

  • • Basic ability to read Arabic script
  • • Dedication of at least 30 minutes daily for practice
  • • A quiet space for memorization and recitation
  • • A copy of the Quran (physical or digital)
